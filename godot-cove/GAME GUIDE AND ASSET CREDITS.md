
Open `godot-cove/project.godot` in Godot 4.7.2 and press F5. Choose **Play Solo** to start or resume your solo trip. On the bow outside Deep River Marina, click the game window to look around. **E** at the wheel takes or leaves the helm. Hold **W/S** to drive forward or reverse, release to coast, and use **A/D** to steer. The outboard turns with the wheel and produces prop wash, cooling water, and RPM-dependent sound. **Tab** opens the chart and journal. Use **1–7** for hotbar slots, **I** for Backpack, **J** for the Fish Index, and **F3** for the FPS counter.

Rich walks a loop on the bow deck, checking the fish finder, looking over the rail, and inspecting his wrench. Look at him and press **E** to stop him for a story. He has 50 voiced lines and leaves roughly two minutes between casual remarks. Subtitles appear nearby, and the host shares his patrol and lines with the crew. His voice was synthesized offline from an original script using Kokoro. [Kokoro model and credits](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M).

Open the **Boats** sign at the Deep River launch to spawn the Princecraft Holiday DLX, Classic, or Panorama. At the helm, aim at the dash key and hold **E** until the white ring fills. Release **E**, then tap it at the key to shut down and coast. Tap or hold **R/F** to set a steady throttle. Holding **W/S** takes over from that setting, and releasing the key returns the throttle to neutral. Steer with **A/D**. The wheel holds its angle when you let go. Hold **X** to center it. Big Rich sits beside you on the Princecraft and has voiced lines for boarding, picking up speed, and slowing to idle.

The Visitor Dock has two fuel pumps. Press **E** to take a nozzle and connect it to the red tank on a stopped boat. Hold the left mouse button to dispense. Press **E** to disconnect and return the nozzle to the pump you took it from. The Marina Attendant at the stand can talk you through it.

The Princecraft ignition uses **Boat Outboard Motor 02** by SoundJay.com under the SoundJay game-use terms. The startup was trimmed to the crank and catch, with the crank repeated to cover the key hold and both clips mixed to mono for stern playback. [Source recording](https://www.soundjay.com/boat-sound-effects.html) · [SoundJay terms of use](https://www.soundjay.com/tos.html)

Open **B → River → Go to landing** on your first trip. Walk down the pier to the cabin plot and press **B** to claim it and place a cabin. Aim at the ground, use **Q** to rotate, and left-click to build. **E** opens the door. Slow down within 22 m of a marked berth and press **L** to tie up and step ashore. Press **L** at the landing to board again.

The **Jobs** page has fishing, freight, surveys, rescue tows, and salvage. Check in at the listed landing to collect timber, parts, and cash. A local depth survey opens ferry service to Swisha and Chalk River. Build cabins at two landings to open a supply line for lower supply prices and better freight pay. Boats, landings, construction, inventory, and active jobs are saved between sessions.

Build a workshop to fabricate the bilge pump, battery bank, winch, electric skiff, and crane barge. A current turbine powers the dry dock and charges boats at the landing. A powered dry dock unlocks the hydrofoil and tethered survey sub. Use **V** near the recorder buoy to launch the fitted sub. **WASD** steers, **Space/Ctrl** changes depth, **E** collects the recorder, and **V** returns to the boat.

For a crew of up to four, choose **Multiplayer** on the title screen. One player selects **Host**. The other players enter the host's LAN or Tailscale IP and select **Join**. The lobby shows everyone connected. The host selects **Start Trip** when the crew is ready. The host's multiplayer world has its own save. Anyone can take the wheel when it is free. The **Crew** page of the boat notebook shows the crew and its records. Use **Pause** or **Crew** to leave the session.

Choose **B → River → Evening at Pine Reach** to start at the shore camp. On the boat, aim at the landing net or ice chest and press **E** to pick it up. Look at a crewmate and press **E** to hand it over. **Q** sets it down, and **E** at its rack secures it. With the net in your hands, aim at a crewmate's fish when it surfaces within reach and left-click to scoop it.

Press **E** at the bow spotlight switch and aim it with the mouse. **E** releases the light at its current angle, and **Q** switches it off while you are aiming. **N** switches between daylight and dusk. Keep a landed fish with **H**, then follow the lanterns at Pine Reach. Use **E** at the grill to place, turn, and serve the fish. **P** saves a local photo and puts the latest shot on the camp's photo board.

Fish from the deck at low speed or from a landing. Press **G** to cycle rigs before casting. Aim at the water and tap **R** to cast. Tap **R** when the float goes under to set the hook. Hold **R** to reel and ease off while the fish pulls line out. Right mouse lifts the rod and the mouse wheel adjusts the drag. Tap **R** when the fish is within net range, then tap **R** again to release it. The journal records species, length, and estimated weight.

The galley is behind the helm. With the boat stopped, aim at the fridge handle and press **E** to open it. Aim at a Molson Canadian or Miller Lite and press **E** to take and drink one. The effects build gradually with each beer and can lead to nausea or a short blackout followed by a hangover. The bong and dab pen on the counter also use **E** and start a 96-second high after their animations. [Molson Canadian](https://www.molson.ca/en-CA/our-brews) · [Miller Lite](https://www.millerlite.com/our-beer)

When the nausea cue appears, you can aim over the rail. Look at a spill on the boat and hold **E** to wipe it up. An overboard spill in fishable water can draw a trophy muskie. Watch for it to roll, cast near the slick, and let it run when the drag clicks. The line has a 10% chance of holding through its final run. A successful hookup still needs to be brought alongside and netted.

Press **F** for a larger view of the helm’s fish finder. The stern transducer reports depth to the modeled bottom and plots returns from simulated schools inside its beam. Auto range follows the bottom, and engine and speed readouts come from the boat. Moving over a shoal or along the channel changes the bottom trace. The model uses the mapped shoreline and published reach descriptions to estimate the submerged terrain. Depths and habitat are game estimates. [Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources Ottawa River resource review](https://ottawariverkeeper.ca/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/tim_haxton_report.pdf)

Press **J** to inspect the 91 modeled species in the Ottawa River fish guide. The playable reach simulates 62 species, including three observable species that the tackle leaves alone. Rig, bait depth, habitat and nearby schools affect which of the other 59 fish bites. The guide labels historical and other-reach records. [Ottawa Riverkeeper main-stem species tables](https://ottawariverkeeper.ca/list-of-fish-species-in-the-ottawa-river/) · [Ontario fish identification guide](https://www.ontario.ca/page/learn-fish-guide)

One Godot unit represents one metre for the boat, shoreline, elevations, buildings, and chart distances. Swisha is about 18 km from the marina by the chart's straight-line measure. The dam is approached from downstream and the Chalk River Labs marker is offshore. [OpenStreetMap shoreline and town features](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/19445112) · [Natural Resources Canada elevation](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/science-data/science-research/geomatics/download-directory-documentation) · [Des Joachims Generating Station](https://water.opg.com/sites/des-joachims-generating-station/)

Deep River's buildings follow OpenStreetMap and [Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints](https://github.com/microsoft/GlobalMLBuildingFootprints) (ODbL). The roof textures and ground colours along the waterfront come from [Geospatial Ontario orthophotography](https://data.ontario.ca/en/dataset/open-ontario-imagery) under the Open Government Licence – Ontario. The roof profiles use Natural Resources Canada terrain and surface elevations. The façade colours and architectural details for Town Hall, the library, the Hexagon clubhouse, and the downtown shopfronts use public photos. Other walls use estimated finishes and colours. [Town Hall roof inspection](https://media-assets-eng.zencity.io/builder-documents/c098deb0-143b-443f-a633-6f37ec67dd37-1774404488148-24ITA045_Town_of_Deep_River_Town_Hall___WTP_DEEP_RIVER_Report.pdf), [Library strategic plan](https://www.deepriverlibrary.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Deep-River-Public-Library-Strategic-Plan-Summary.pdf), [Shoreline Sports Club](https://www.drssc.ca/about), [Downtown photos by Bob and Diana McElroy](https://mcelroy.ca/news/archive/20200415ps.html).

Deep River's street names and mapped pavement classifications use the [Ontario Road Network Composite](https://data.ontario.ca/dataset/ontario-road-network-orn-composite). Contains information licensed under the [Open Government Licence – Ontario](https://www.ontario.ca/page/open-government-licence-ontario). The gravel ranges in `ottawa/deep_river_road_details.json` are measured along the existing game centerlines. Its junction entries give the intersection center and the label and heading of each street blade. Crack patterns and exact signpost offsets are game art. Google Earth imagery was used as a layout reference.

The rows in `ottawa/municipal_traffic_controls.json` identify the sign type, approach street, intersecting street, in-game approach anchor and direction toward incoming traffic. Stop and yield approaches follow the schedules in [Deep River By-law 37-2012](https://deepriver.civicweb.net/document/4194) where the roads can be matched to the game. May 2025 Street View shows the [Silvie Street stop at Hillcrest](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=7qfPUDHj2kD7a94P_TP_FA&heading=104), [Laurier school crossing](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=REsuD-mZNJ9Ez5QlOJ92LQ&heading=3), and [Deep River Road pedestrian crossover](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=BrdllzLQr-zBT_rWbuw4Dw&heading=56). Deep River identified Laurier near Brockhouse Way as the [school crossing location](https://www.deepriver.ca/userfiles/file/newsletter/October-December_newsletter.pdf). The Ontario Wc-2 face is [public domain](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:CA-ON_road_sign_Wc-002.svg).

Roadside sidewalk ranges follow the [town sidewalk asset map, figure A-4](https://www.deepriver.ca/userfiles/file/Asset%20Management%20Plan%20v1%2020220630%281%29.pdf#page=51) and the [town road and sidewalk plan](https://www.deepriver.ca/projects/municipal-projects/project-archive/2022-short-term-road-and-sidewalk-plan-8879.html). The [Glendale approach toward Champlain](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=VKFIbikd_jLaFe_V3v7X0w&heading=16.17) and the [reverse view toward the residential blocks](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=VKFIbikd_jLaFe_V3v7X0w&heading=167) show the concrete curbs and detached asphalt sidewalk. In `ottawa/sidewalk_edges.json`, each [road index](ottawa/river.json) has rows with start and end distances in metres, side relative to the road's stored direction, grass setback, walkway width, material, and a curb flag. A zero walkway width gives a curb-only edge. The curb top, verge, and walkway share a height at ordinary curbed sections. Driveway and crosswalk ramps lower the curb, and the outer verge blends into the existing terrain. The inventory also shows detached paths whose routes do not align with a playable road.

To rebuild the included map assets, run `.venv\Scripts\python.exe download_ottawa_town.py` to fetch the town imagery, surface and terrain tiles, and supplemental footprints. Then run `.venv\Scripts\python.exe generate_ottawa_fishing.py`. The generator also uses the shoreline and regional elevation data already in `data/ottawa_fishing` and the OpenStreetMap features in `data/deep_river.gpkg`.

The marina uses the basin, breakwall, docks, and Pier Park footprint traced from [Geospatial Ontario aerial imagery](https://data.ontario.ca/en/dataset/open-ontario-imagery). The tracing is in `marina_layout.json`. The terrain bake resamples a one-metre [Natural Resources Canada HRDEM Mosaic terrain tile](https://open.canada.ca/data/dataset/0fe65119-e96e-4a57-8bfe-9d9245fba06b) on a 2 m grid and uses the raster's estimated local water level of 111.606 m as the zero reference. The source tile is `data/ottawa_fishing/marina_elevation.tif`. The wave model drives the surface, hull and float. Shallow water shows the refracted bed and moving light, while the engine and moving hull disturb the surface behind the stern.

Town tree positions and heights are estimated from the difference between the HRDEM surface and bare-earth rasters. The Pier Park pavilion and playground are game models based on the town's photos and aerial imagery. [Town of Deep River recreation and parks overview](https://www.deepriver.ca/userfiles/file/Attachment%201%20-%202022%20Recreation%20Parks%20and%20Facilities%20Overview.pdf#page=12).

The public wharf, launch dock, and marina shack use footprints traced from the marina aerials. The red harbour light uses the charted wharf corner and flashes every four seconds. [Canadian Coast Guard listing for Deep River Harbour, light 1313](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2025/mpo-dfo/Fs151-6-2-2025-06-eng.pdf).

The marina includes eight sample boats in the mapped slips. Their 6–8 m hulls, sailboat masts, fenders, and canopies reflect the waterfront photos. They move with the waves and occupy the space inside their respective slips. [Town of Deep River photo gallery](https://www.deepriver.ca/about/photo-gallery/).

The grooved concrete launch lies between the timber boarding dock and the public wharf. The ramp uses the charted 9.4 m width, with a game-modeled grade extending below the waterline. [Canadian Hydrographic Service sailing directions, chapter 6](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2022/mpo-dfo/Fs74-28-2021-12-eng.pdf).

Press **4** to set a waypoint for Lamure Beach. The sandy outline follows the Ontario aerial imagery, and the land elevations come from the NRCan bare-earth tile sampled every 2 m. The shallow riverbed is modeled. The beach includes the lifeguard stands, picnic shelter, blue access mat, spiral slide, swing set, volleyball net, and swim float. Wade inside the buoy line and press **E** at the float's ladder to climb onto it. [Town of Deep River parks overview](https://www.deepriver.ca/userfiles/file/Attachment%201%20-%202022%20Recreation%20Parks%20and%20Facilities%20Overview.pdf#page=20), [Open Water Data beach description](https://www.openwaterdata.com/site/plage-lamure-beach).

The surface scans in `godot-cove/ottawa/materials` and the tone-mapped daytime sky use Poly Haven CC0 assets. [Grass](https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass), [asphalt](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_03), [concrete](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_02), [brick](https://polyhaven.com/a/brick_wall_02), [wood](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_planks_04), [sand](https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_sand_01), [daylight panorama](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky).

The unpaved roads and shoulders use Amal Kumar's two-metre [Gravel Road](https://polyhaven.com/a/gravel_road) scan from Poly Haven (CC0).

Lamure’s pine materials use CC0 assets from Poly Haven and Yughues. [Pine bark](https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_bark), [forest floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/forrest_ground_03), [pine branch textures](https://opengameart.org/content/branches-textures-pack-02).

Swisha uses a 4 m terrain grid, a raised headpond, and a powerhouse below the concrete crest. The plan is traced from the Ontario aerial tile in `swisha_layout.json`. The source rasters are `data/ottawa_fishing/swisha_imagery.tif` and `data/ottawa_fishing/swisha_elevation.tif`. The elevation tile puts the headpond at about 150.9 m and the tailrace at about 112.8 m. The eight animated outlets, the foam, and the cascade audio are game effects. [Ontario Power Generation station overview](https://water.opg.com/sites/des-joachims-generating-station/)

## Zero Turn controls

While seated, use W/S to drive, A/D to steer, Q/R to lower or raise the throttle, X for the blades, C to change the camera view, and E to dismount. The throttle indicator shows Low, Normal, High, or Transport.

## Public Works and mowing

Follow Cedar Road to the Public Works yard. Open the bay or office doors with E when prompted. Speak to Shannon in the office to get the current mowing route, take either Zero Turn, and use the route markers to find the grass to cut. Return to Shannon to collect payment.
## Houseboat, regional travel, and graphics

The extended houseboat has an aft bedroom and bathroom. Aim at the bed or toilet and press **E**. Press **E** or **Space** to get up, and **F** to flush when seated. Nearby crew see the poses and hear the sounds. Press **E** at the TV to toggle power. The shared loop shows a placeholder until licensed media is installed. See [TV media instructions](ottawa/tv/README.txt).

Open the chart with **Tab** and choose the entire river or an 18 × 18 km Deep River, Swisha, or Chalk River section. Use the destination list to fast travel. McAnulty Road, the cemetery footprint, and ski club and Grouse Park markers use data from [OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL 1.0](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The helipad, generic grave markers, ski trail connection, and raised Swisha approach are gameplay adaptations.

At the McAnulty helipad, **E** boards the four-seat survey helicopter. The first rider pilots with **WASD**, turns with **Q/Z**, and climbs or descends with **Space/Ctrl**. Land and slow down before using **E** to exit. Use **Esc > Unstuck** for emergency recovery. The helicopter model, rotor sound, TV tone, and 20 flush sounds are original procedural assets.

Use **F4** or **Esc > Settings > Graphics quality / distance** to choose Performance, Balanced, or Scenic. The presets vary scenery and shadows, water detail, and 2×/4×/8× MSAA.
